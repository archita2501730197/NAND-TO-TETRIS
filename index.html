          Individual Viva Voce Questions & Model Answers
        

        

          

            Q1: How does Memory.hdl direct addresses?

            Bits 14 and 13 decode address space: 00/01 access 16K RAM, 10 accesses 8K Screen map (16384-24575), and 11 accesses the Keyboard register at 24576.
          

          

            Q2: How does the two-pass assembler handle symbols?

            Pass 1 records labels (LABEL) with their ROM instruction index. Pass 2 resolves labels and assigns new variables sequentially to RAM registers starting at 16.
          

          

            Q3: What was the bug you encountered and how did you fix it?

            Hack ALU specifies D+M as the valid mnemonic, not M+D. The assembler caught it via the $K \bmod 3 = 1$ feature. Also, pointer resetting was placed inside (RENDER) to prevent vertical drifting.
          

          

            Q4: How do you move the box down during viva?

            Change base address @19488 to @(19488 + 32 * rows). E.g., shifting down 10 rows means updating to @19808.
